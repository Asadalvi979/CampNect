Riphah International University, Sahiwal Campus is committed to providing quality education and fostering academic excellence. To strengthen communication, collaboration, and knowledge sharing among students, seniors, and alumni, CampNect has been developed as a centralized platform where users can connect, share resources, participate in academic discussions, and build meaningful professional networks within the university community.

Key Features of CampNect

Everything students need to connect, collaborate, and succeed in one platform.

Notes Sharing

Upload, organize, and access academic notes and study materials from fellow students.

Real-Time Messaging

Communicate instantly with classmates, seniors, and authorized alumni members.

Project Collaboration

Find teammates, share project ideas, and work together on academic initiatives.

Academic Communities

Join department and interest-based communities to discuss ideas and stay connected.

Alumni Connections

Build professional relationships and seek guidance from experienced graduates.

Announcements

Stay updated with academic notices, university events, and important deadlines.

Why CampNect?

Bridging the gap between academic needs and digital solutions

What Students Face Today

  • Multiple Disconnected Platforms

    Students use WhatsApp, Google Drive, LMS, and emails separately — scattered resources and missed updates.

  • Communication Gaps

    No structured way to reach seniors for guidance or collaborate with peers on projects.

  • Scattered Study Resources

    Notes and materials are shared randomly, often lost in chats or forgotten folders.

  • Limited Networking Opportunities

    Students miss out on mentorship, alumni connections, and project collaborations.

How CampNect Solves It

  • All-in-One Platform

    Chat, resources, announcements, and collaboration — everything under one roof.

  • Semester-Based Communication

    Structured access: Juniors connect with seniors, seniors guide juniors, alumni mentor all.

  • Centralized Resource Hub

    Upload, download, and organize study materials by semester and subject.

  • Built-in Alumni Network

    Direct mentorship, career guidance, and industry insights from graduates.

Eligibility & Access Requirements

Who Can Join CampNect?

  • Must Be a Riphah Student Users must possess a valid CMS Number issued by Riphah International University.
  • Verified Registration All accounts must be registered using authentic university information for identity verification.
  • Academic Use Only The platform is intended for academic collaboration, networking, knowledge sharing, and professional development.
  • Respect Community Guidelines Users must communicate respectfully and avoid abusive, misleading, or inappropriate content.
  • Alumni Access Alumni members can join the platform to mentor students, share experiences, and provide career guidance.
  • Secure Communication Access to certain features and communication channels depends on the user's academic level and platform permissions.

Ready to Become Part of the CampNect Community?
